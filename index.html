Getting around

This dashboard shows EnviroVac training records in one place. It refreshes itself early every morning from HSI, TAPPISAFE and the site requirements list, so what you see is as of this morning. The time of the last refresh is at the top right.

  • Filters under the tabs (Employee, Location, Role, Course) narrow what you see on Site Specific, HSI Training and Overview. Course only shows on Site Specific and Overview; HSI Training has its own requirement picker. Pick as many as you like in each; click Clear filters to start over. Filters are hidden on Site Requirements and How to Use, where they don't apply.
  • Click any name to open a panel on the right with everything that person has on file: site readiness, HSI training and all completed courses.
  • Click a column heading to sort by it; click again to reverse.
  • Export CSV downloads exactly what the table is showing, filters included, to open in Excel.
  • Your filters and tab are remembered on your own device only. Nobody else sees them change.

Site Specific

What it holds: every training record from the HSI Training Summary plus the plant-site orientations from TAPPISAFE (marked with a TAPPI tag). One row per person per course, with location, role, completed date, expiration date, days left and Length (how long the course takes to complete, from the HSI hours reference sheet, the TAPPISAFE course list and the site training times list; ISNetworld site courses use the ISNetworld site times when the site has only that one course; a course at a site with several courses stays blank until its own time is known, since pay is figured per course taken. Courses with no time to pay show why instead: Title Only, Document or Medical).

Views: Training records lists each course record. By employee shows one row per person with a count of courses in each status.

Status colors

  • Current: completed and good for more than 90 days (or never expires).
  • ≤30 days: expires within the next 30 days.
  • 31–60 days: expires in 31 to 60 days.
  • 61–90 days: expires in 61 to 90 days.
  • Expired: the expiration date has passed.
  • Failed: the last attempt was not passed.
  • Not completed: assigned in HSI but not finished.
  • No training: the person has no training on file.

Rows that are expired or expiring are shaded in their color so they stand out. Click a status button above the table to show only that status; click more than one to combine.

Tags and notes

  • retake the newest completion of a course that was taken more than once. Older attempts are hidden unless you tick Show older records replaced by a retake; they then show as replaced.
  • Medical - Not a Course fit tests, blood panels, medical exams, PFT, audiograms and Rapid MEQ. These are medical requirements, not classes.
  • For Level Up Program cohort requirements ending in (4m), (8m) or (1yr), and the Tech II to Tech III Skill Assessment.

Plant-site readiness (green / yellow / red lights)

Pick a mill's course in the Course filter (for example a Mansfield orientation). A Site column appears with one light per person:

  • Ready: has TAPPISAFE Basic or Basic Refresher, the mill orientation and, if the mill has one, the PSM orientation, all passed and not expired.
  • Ready, expires within 30 days: everything is in place but something runs out within 30 days.
  • Not ready: something is missing, expired or failed.

The bar above the table counts Ready / Expiring / Not ready; click one to show only those people. Click a name to see exactly what they are missing for that site.

HSI Training

What it holds: the HSI Training Requirement Status report: every required (recurring) training for every active member, with last completed date, when the training window opens, the due date and days until due.

Compliance group: pick one group at a time, because each follows its own rules: Refresher Training (annual safety refreshers and the rest), Level Up Trainings (the (4m), (8m) and (1yr) cohort items and the Tech II to Tech III Skill Assessment) or Medical Trainings (fit tests, blood panels, exams, PFT, audiogram, Rapid MEQ). The drop-down next to it narrows to one requirement.

Status colors

  • Delinquent: past due in HSI.
  • In Training Window: the window to retake it is open and it is due soon.
  • Current: up to date.
  • Current - next not assigned: up to date; the next round has not been assigned yet (its window opens after the due date shown).

The Delinquent or in training window button shows everything that needs attention in the chosen group; Current shows the rest.

Site Requirements

What it holds: one card per customer site from the Vecta and EnviroVac Training Site Specific Requirements list: division, training platform, site specific training and how many, basic training, training location and link, background check, drug screen, fit test, other requirements, specific directions and attached documents.

  • Search any word (for example TWIC or PFT) and filter by Division, Training platform or Background check. Not listed shows sites with no background check entered.
  • Site training time is how long the site's training takes (from the training department's site times list), and each site specific training shows its own time in ( ) when known.
  • Specific directions leave out any sentence about usernames, passwords or logins; ask the training department for those.
  • Attachments open from the Microsoft List, so you need access to the list to open them.
  • Changes made in the list show up here the next morning.

Overview

What it holds: a summary of the Site Specific records. The tiles count records in each status (same colors as above); click a tile to filter by it. Status by location shows a bar per location split into the status colors; click a location to filter to it.

Where the data comes from

  • HSI Training Summary and HSI Training Requirement Status: pulled from HSI each morning.
  • TAPPISAFE student attendance: pulled each morning.
  • Site requirements: the Microsoft List kept by the training department.

Something look wrong or missing? Contact the EnviroVac training department (envirovactraining@envirovac.us).

Statuses are worked out from the expiration date in the HSI export (and, for plant-site courses, the TAPPISAFE export). When an employee has retaken a course, the newest completion counts and older records for that course are hidden unless you turn them on in Site Specific. Your filters are your own; nobody else sees them change.
